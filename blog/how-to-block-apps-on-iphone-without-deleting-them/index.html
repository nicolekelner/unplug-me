How to block apps on iPhone without deleting them

Keep your accounts and messages while making distracting apps harder to open.

By Nicole Kelner · October 7, 2026

You can block or limit distracting iPhone apps without deleting them. Start with Apple’s built-in Screen Time controls if a schedule or daily limit is enough. If you want a physical boundary, Unplug Me can keep distracting apps locked until you connect your charger.

Start with the apps you want to keep

Write down two or three apps that pull you in most often. Keep apps you truly need, such as calls, messages, maps, and banking, out of the experiment. The goal is to change access to distracting apps while preserving useful parts of your phone.

If you use a social app to message friends, decide whether you need the entire app available or whether you could check messages at a planned time.

Try Apple Screen Time first

On iPhone, Screen Time lets you set daily App Limits and schedule Downtime. You can choose apps and contacts that remain available during Downtime. Apple’s settings can be enough if a clock-based rule fits your life.

A daily limit answers “How long?” Downtime answers “When?” Both are different from deciding where you can open an app. See Apple’s Screen Time instructions for current setup steps.

Add a physical boundary if time limits are too easy to ignore

Unplug Me uses charging as the cue. You select the apps to keep available, then plug in to unlock distracting apps for a timed session. Unplugging or reaching the timer’s end locks them again.

This is useful when you want access at home but less temptation everywhere you carry your phone. Keep your charger in a place where you are comfortable using those apps.

Test the setup on an ordinary day

Before a trip or a busy workday, make sure the essentials you need are available. Try one day, notice what is frustrating, and adjust your choices. Stronger restrictions are only useful if they fit your real obligations.

If you need temporary access away from a charger, review the emergency unlock options in your installed version of Unplug Me.

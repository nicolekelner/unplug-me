If a timer or schedule is enough

Start with Apple Screen Time. Its App Limits and Downtime are already on your iPhone. Opal, ScreenZen, Freedom, and Jomo add different ways to schedule or customize blocking.

If you need a moment to reconsider

one sec focuses on interrupting an automatic app open. Unplug Me uses the action of plugging in, then a timed session. They create different kinds of pauses: one happens at the screen, the other at a charger.

If you want a separate object

Brick uses a dedicated device you tap with your phone. Unplug Me uses a charger you already have. Consider where you want the object to live and how you would handle an unexpected need for access.

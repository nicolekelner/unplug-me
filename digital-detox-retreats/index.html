RETREAT GUIDE / UNITED STATES

Digital detox retreats
in America.

From quiet meditation centers to flexible wellness programs, find the kind of break that actually fits your life.

Based on Free Time’s U.S. retreat guide, first published June 2025. Center details may have changed; confirm directly before booking.

A good retreat matches the amount of disconnection you want. Before choosing a center, ask whether phones are prohibited, discouraged, or simply less central to the experience. Then check the program schedule, travel needs, and how someone can reach you in an emergency.

Start with the phone rule

“Digital detox” covers several different experiences. A silent retreat might ask you to put devices away for the full program. A resort might limit phones only in shared spaces. A remote site might have poor cell service but no formal rule. Ask the center which policy applies to the exact dates and program you plan to book.

Northeast: meditation, yoga, and longer stays

The Northeast includes structured meditation centers as well as more flexible yoga and learning programs. If you want a first retreat without a strict no-phone rule, compare program descriptions carefully. If you want silence, ask exactly how the center handles devices.

  • Kripalu Center for Yoga & Health — Stockbridge, MA (Berkshires)
  • Insight Meditation Society (IMS) — Barre, MA
  • Sivananda Ashram Yoga Ranch — Woodbourne, NY (Catskills)

Southeast: smaller centers and mountain settings

North Carolina has options for both a guided wellness weekend and a quieter residential meditation retreat. Check whether the phone rule applies across the whole campus or only inside the program.

  • Art of Living Retreat Center — Boone, NC (Blue Ridge Mountains)
  • Southern Dharma Retreat Center — Hot Springs, NC

California: coast, forest, and silence

California’s range makes the retreat format especially important. A workshop at Esalen, a silent residential retreat at Spirit Rock, and a program at 1440 Multiversity can have very different schedules and device expectations.

  • Esalen Institute — Big Sur, CA
  • Spirit Rock Meditation Center — Woodacre, CA (Marin County)
  • 1440 Multiversity — Scotts Valley, CA (Santa Cruz Mountains)

Pacific Northwest: forests and off-grid stays

Some programs emphasize meditation; others emphasize nature and time away. Ask about coverage, Wi-Fi, emergency contact procedures, and travel access before assuming the setting is fully disconnected.

  • Breitenbush Hot Springs — Detroit, OR (Cascade Range)
  • Cloud Mountain Retreat Center — Castle Rock, WA

Mountain West and Southwest: practice and wide-open space

Colorado, New Mexico, Arizona, and Montana offer everything from Buddhist practice to a resort stay. Match the intensity of the program to your goals rather than assuming every “retreat” means the same level of silence.

  • Drala Mountain Center (formerly Shambhala Mountain Center) — Red Feather Lakes, CO
  • Upaya Zen Center — Santa Fe, NM
  • Miraval Arizona Resort & Spa — Tucson, AZ

Before you book

  • Ask whether you must surrender your phone, may keep it in your room, or can use it in designated areas.
  • Confirm price, lodging, meals, accessibility, refund rules, and travel from the nearest airport or station.
  • Give family the center’s emergency contact details and decide how you will handle important work or care responsibilities.
  • Check the current program calendar. A center’s general policy may differ from a specific retreat leader’s rules.

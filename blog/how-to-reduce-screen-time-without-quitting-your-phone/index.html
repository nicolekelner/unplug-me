How to reduce screen time without quitting your phone

A realistic plan for using your iPhone less while keeping the parts that help you.

By Nicole Kelner · October 7, 2026

To reduce screen time, focus on one repeatable scrolling moment rather than trying to stop using your phone altogether. Measure that moment, remove a few prompts, and put a boundary between you and the app you open automatically.

Find the time you want back

Look at your iPhone’s Screen Time report and identify one app or category you would like to use less. Then ask when you tend to open it. The number matters less than the situation: after waking, during a break, or just before bed.

Choose a narrow target such as “no social feeds in bed” or “one intentional check after lunch.” That is easier to test than “use my phone less.”

Separate useful screen time from accidental screen time

A map, a call with a friend, and a video feed all count as screen time, but they serve different purposes. Keep the tools you rely on. Focus your change on sessions that run longer than you meant them to.

If your work happens on a screen, total hours may be a poor measure of progress. Notice whether you are making a choice or following a habit.

Remove prompts, then add a pause

Turn off notifications that mostly invite you back to an app. Move that app away from the first Home Screen. Decide what you want before you open it. A short pause is often enough to notice that you were reaching out of habit.

For a stronger cue, Unplug Me lets you keep distracting apps locked until you plug in, then choose a timed session. It gives opening the app a deliberate first step.

Review after a week

Check whether the target moment feels different and whether you kept access to the things you need. If the boundary feels too hard to live with, adjust its time or location. If it is too easy to ignore, move the charger farther from the places you tend to scroll.

There is no universal number of “good” screen-time hours. The useful measure is whether your phone is helping you do what you intended.
